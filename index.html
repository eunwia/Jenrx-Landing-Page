Terms of Service & Privacy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Local-First Architecture

1. Local-First Storage & Data Ownership

Your health information is deeply personal. Jenrx is engineered with a strict local-first architecture. Every medication log, blood pressure reading, clinic appointment, and medical document PDF you store remains exclusively on your Android device's local storage.

We do not transmit, upload, or synchronize your personal health records to centralized cloud databases or third-party servers. If you delete the app or clear its storage, your records are permanently erased from the device.

2. Zero Data Selling & No Advertising SDKs

We believe health tracking should never be monetized through surveillance. Jenrx operates under a clear, uncompromising commitment:

  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, trade, or share your identity or medical data with insurers, pharmaceutical companies, or data brokers.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: The Android application contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics pixels, or behavioral tracking libraries.
  • No User Profiling: We do not construct behavioral profiles or target you with sponsored medical products.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Jenrx requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to provide essential tracking functions:

  • Storage / Media Access: Strictly used to allow you to select, preview, and organize your medical test results and prescription documents inside your custom folders.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used exclusively to deliver your scheduled daily dosage prompts and appointment reminders locally on your device.

4. Data Export & Control

You maintain 100% ownership of your data at all times. You can export your health logs into standard readable formats or wipe all stored records instantly through the in-app settings.

1. Personal Wellness Companion Disclaimer

Jenrx is provided strictly as an informational and personal wellness organization aid. It is designed to assist you in keeping track of your daily prescription schedules, blood pressure logs, and clinical appointment dates.

Important Medical Disclaimer: Jenrx is NOT a medical device, licensed medical practitioner, clinical diagnostic tool, or emergency health service. It cannot diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider regarding any questions you have about a medical condition, prescription dosage, or health symptoms. Never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice because of information tracked within Jenrx.

2. User Responsibility for Dosage Accuracy

While Jenrx provides tools to log and schedule medications, you are solely responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the medicine names, dosages, intervals, and doctor consultation times that you enter into the application.

3. Software License & Availability

Jenrx is licensed for personal, non-commercial use. The Android APK is made available "as is" and "as available". We continuously test and optimize the application to ensure stability, reliability, and privacy across supported Android operating system versions.

4. Updates & Contact

We may update these terms as new features are introduced. Continued use of Jenrx constitutes acceptance of the latest terms. For inquiries regarding privacy or software feedback, reach out to our team at support@jenrx.app.
